BSCS Simulation & Modeling — Technical Specification

Hybrid 3D Agent-Based & PDE Reaction-Diffusion Tumor Model

Educational Model Only

CancerSim implements a multiscale, discrete-time ($\Delta t = 1\text{ hour}$) hybrid computational biology model decoupling a 3D continuous spatial voxel grid ($12 \times 12 \times 12$ nodes across $[-15, 15]^3$ spatial units) from an Agent-Based Model (ABM) of individual eukaryotic cells. This architecture captures emergent tumor spheroid phenomena including avascular growth saturation, peripheral proliferation rims, and central hypoxic/necrotic cores.

1. 3D Reaction-Diffusion Microenvironment

Diffusible species $C_s(\mathbf{x}, t)$ for Glucose ($s=G$), Oxygen ($s=O_2$), Stress ($s=S$), and Cytotoxic Drug ($s=D$) evolve via explicit 3D finite-difference Laplacian diffusion with Dirichlet boundary vascular perfusion:

∂C_s/∂t = D_s ∇²C_s − Σ U_{i,s}(C_s) + S_boundary

Cellular metabolic uptake follows saturating Michaelis-Menten kinetics: $U = V_{\max} \frac{C}{K_m + C}$, where cancer cells exhibit a $2.6\times$ Warburg glycolytic uptake multiplier.

2. Stochastic Mitotic Proliferation

At each discrete time step $\Delta t$, viable cells evaluate mitotic division probability $P_{\text{div}}$ governed by local nutrient/oxygen sufficiency, cell cycle maturity ($A_i \ge A_{\min}$), and local neighbor crowding $N_{\text{nbr}}$:

P_div = k_base · M(G, O₂) · (1 − N_nbr / N_max)

Healthy cells enforce strict contact inhibition ($N_{\max} = 6$), whereas malignant cells have mutated contact inhibition ($N_{\max} = 18$), driving multilayered 3D tumor clustering.

3. Hypoxic Necrosis & Cytotoxic Apoptosis

Cells transition to a DEAD state stochastically when local $O_2$ or Glucose drops below critical metabolic thresholds (ischemic necrosis) or in response to environmental stress and therapeutic agents:

P_death = p_0 + α_hyp·H(O₂) + α_str·S + α_drug·D

Because rapidly dividing cancer rims consume oxygen before it diffuses inward, the tumor interior naturally develops a realistic necrotic core.

4. Biomechanical Repulsion & Motility

Cells interact via short-range elastic steric repulsion when center-to-center distance $d_{ij} < 2R_{\text{cell}}$, preventing non-physical overlap while pushing daughter cells outward during mitosis, combined with weak chemotactic drift along glucose gradients.

Academic & Clinical Scope Disclaimer: CancerSim is constructed exclusively as an educational computational modeling platform for demonstrating Agent-Based Modeling, 3D PDE reaction-diffusion numerical methods, stochastic state machines, and sensitivity analysis. Simplified dimensionless parameters are used; it does not represent a patient-specific clinical prognosis or medical diagnostic tool.
